Medical and non-medical paths

Find the place your skills can help.

01

Medical volunteers

Clinical support

Licensed or appropriately trained volunteers may ask about opportunities that fit their verified credentials and the clinic’s current needs.Clinical roles require clinic approval, credential verification, defined supervision, privacy training, and an approved scope before service begins.
02

Medical volunteers

Health education support

Qualified volunteers may help the clinic prepare or deliver approved health-education activities.No volunteer should provide individual medical advice outside an approved role.
03

Non-medical volunteers

Welcome and outreach

Community members may ask about helping with hospitality, outreach, events, or patient-facing navigation.Available activities depend on current clinic needs and required training.
04

Non-medical volunteers

Practical operations

Organization, resource preparation, language support, and administrative skills can also help care run more clearly.Access to patient or clinic information is limited by privacy rules and assigned duties.
