# Content source register

Clinic facts and service descriptions in the release candidate were reconciled against the clinic's current public website at `puravidacc.org`, including homepage, services, about, patient resources, FAQ, Spanish, and donation pages as available during the August 2026 build.

Patient registration fields were mapped from the clinic-provided `Adult Patient Chart April.pdf`.

No provider credentials, licenses, partnerships, testimonials, tax status, or guaranteed service claims were invented. Any such authority content should be added only from clinic-verified records.
