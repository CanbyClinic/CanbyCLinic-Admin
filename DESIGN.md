# Canby cinematic design system

## Creative idea

The website is one continuous visit: arrive, cross the threshold, understand care, prepare, and follow through. Photography and spatial motion carry the narrative; the three-dimensional geometry represents recognizable clinic architecture, objects, and routes rather than abstract decoration.

## Brand rules

- Use the supplied Canby Community Clinic / Pura Vida Community Clinic lockup unchanged.
- Do not redraw, crop, distort, recolor, rotate, or separate the symbol from the wordmark.
- Use Canby blue `#084EB0` for navigation and action, Canby green `#10940B` for assurance and progress, deep clinic ink `#07131B` for the cinematic stage, and warm paper `#F2EFE7` for readable information.
- Keep a white protection field behind the supplied JPEG logo so its white background remains intentional.
- Typography is editorial and direct: a large condensed display face built from the local system stack, neutral sans-serif body copy, and monospace wayfinding labels.

## Motion language

- Home: approach and threshold crossing.
- Services: consultation view opens into a four-bay care system.
- Appointments: physical paired doors reveal the three appointment steps.
- New patients: a preparation table advances toward the viewer.
- Resources: a tactile rack of differently colored folders indexes practical information.
- Visit: a road and destination perspective create an approach to Reseda.
- Our clinic: the team image stays calm while the mission copy moves in restrained editorial beats.
- Route transitions: paired architectural doors close and open in under one second.

Movement uses transforms and opacity where possible. The persistent WebGL canvas is isolated behind readable HTML. Reduced-motion mode hides the canvas, removes spatial choreography, displays the route poster, and preserves the full reading order.

## Media rules

The seven route scenes are distinct, original AI-generated representative images. They are not photos of actual patients, staff, or the Canby facility and are never presented as documentary evidence. No scene is reused on another narrative route. The supplied logo is the only externally provided visual asset in the release.

## Release targets

- No sideways page overflow at 390, 768, 1024, or 1440 CSS pixels.
- Minimum 44-pixel interactive targets.
- Visible focus styles and a skip link.
- All critical contact, eligibility, privacy, and emergency information remains HTML text.
- Progressive enhancement: the site stays usable without WebGL or animation.
