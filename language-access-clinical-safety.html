Language Access & Patient Safety

Why language support, interpretation, and clear instructions can affect safety. This page is written in plain language so patients and families can understand the topic before calling or visiting.

Good health information should help people take the next step. For Canby Clinic, that means preparing questions, bringing useful information, understanding when a concern may be urgent, and knowing where to find community support.

How to prepare

  • Write down your main concern in one sentence.
  • Bring your medication list or bottles if available.
  • Bring recent paperwork, readings, or questions.
  • Ask what follow-up steps may be needed.
Important: This page is educational only and does not diagnose or replace medical advice. For emergencies, call 911.

Questions to ask

  • What should I watch for?
  • When should I follow up?
  • What information should I track at home?
  • Are there community resources that can help?
