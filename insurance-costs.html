Insurance & costs

Bring any current insurance information you have. Insurance details are optional during online account creation and can also be provided directly to clinic staff.

If you have insurance

Bring your current insurance card and photo identification if available. Call the clinic with questions about current billing or coverage procedures.

If you do not have insurance

Do not delay contacting the clinic. Call staff to ask about current eligibility, cost, and available care options.
