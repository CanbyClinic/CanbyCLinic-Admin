Before, During, and After Your Visit

Patient resources

Use these tools to prepare questions, organize medications, understand referrals, and follow up on results without searching through scattered pages.

Before you continue

Three boundaries to remember

Forms

Online forms help with administrative preparation. They are not emergency or diagnosis tools.

Language

The website is available in English, Spanish, and Armenian. Confirm visit-level language assistance by phone.

Follow-up

If you were told to expect a result or referral and it does not arrive in the expected window, contact the clinic rather than assuming silence means normal.

Before You Leave a Visit

Confirm the plan before you leave

  • Repeat the plan in your own words.
  • Confirm medication changes and what should stop.
  • Ask when and how results will be delivered.
  • Write down who is coordinating a referral.
  • Know what symptoms require urgent or emergency care.

This website is not monitored for emergencies. Call 911 in an emergency.
