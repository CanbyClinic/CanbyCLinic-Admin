CANBY COMMUNITY CLINIC — GITHUB PAGES UPLOAD

Upload the CONTENTS of this package to the root of the CanbyCLinic-Admin
repository's publishing branch.

Required at the repository root:
- index.html
- .nojekyll
- _next/
- assets/
- page folders such as services/, appointments/, and visit/

Built for:
https://canbyclinic.github.io/CanbyCLinic-Admin/

Keep .nojekyll. Without it, GitHub Pages may hide the _next folder and the
site will load without its styling or interaction.

Homepage interaction:
Nine original photoreal continuity frames move a physician toward the clinic,
open the two doors, and cross the threshold as the visitor scrolls. The images
are not stock photographs and are not reused elsewhere on the website.
